# Watch guide

Today is Wednesday, so also pick the games most worth watching in the coming week (Wednesday to Tuesday, Melbourne time). It goes in each league's `watch`.

Each league has a list of `watchCandidates` (ids like `nba-g4`), already roughly ordered by records, standings and TV slot. Each shows both teams' records, places and streaks where the season has started.

For each league:

**picks**: the 3 to 5 games most worth watching, best first (fewer only if there are fewer than 3 games). For each, give the game's `id` as `game` and a one-line `reason`, for example "Top two in the West" or "The league's best offence against its best defence".

- Base the reason on the data: records, standings, streaks, stat leaders and the week's headlines (for example a star back from injury or a team's first game after a big trade, if the headlines say so).
- Good reasons: two winning teams, a division or conference rival, a playoff-race game, a star matchup, a team on a long streak, a national TV or prime-time game.
- Don't pick a game just because it's on TV if a better game is available.
- Jack's team's games (`jacksTeam: true`) are always shown to him anyway, so only include them in the picks if they'd rank on merit.
- Don't work out new numbers that aren't in the data.
